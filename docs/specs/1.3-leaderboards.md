## 1. Problem

Players like comparing themselves with the people they play with: who put in the most hours this month, who kept a weekly streak going. Today, the only option is sharing an image (1.1). There's no running, friendly competition inside the app.

Typical leaderboards rank by profit or win rate. That's a bad fit here:
- **Easy to fake.** Results are typed in by the player, so nothing stops someone entering a fake cash-out.
- **Private.** Money is the most sensitive thing in the app, and every earlier spec keeps it off by default.
- **Harmful incentives.** Ranking by winnings rewards bragging and risk-taking.

So 1.3 ranks friends and private groups by **effort and consistency** (hours, sessions, streaks). Those are harder to fake and safer to show.

---

## 2. Goals and non-goals

### Goals
1. Players can compare hours played, sessions and streaks with **friends** and inside **private groups** they choose to join.
2. **Opt-in and private by default.** Nobody appears anywhere until they turn leaderboards on, and then only to people they've connected with.
3. Others see a **display name and the ranked numbers only**. Never money, location, notes, stakes, dates or times of sessions, or seat names.
4. Rankings are **hard to game**, with clear rules for implausible or edited sessions.
5. Meets App Store guideline **1.2** for user-generated content: a filter for objectionable content, reporting with timely response, blocking, and published contact info.
6. Leaving is clean. Opting out or deleting the account removes the player from every board.

### Non-goals for 1.3
- **No global or public leaderboard**, no regional boards, no search for strangers.
- **No money rankings**: no profit, win rate, BB/hr, ROI, or biggest win, even as an opt-in.
- **No chat, comments, reactions, or messaging** of any kind, including group descriptions with free text beyond the group name.
- No profile pages, avatars, photos, or bios. A display name is the only user-generated text, besides the group name.
- No public share links to boards. Invites are covered in section 6.
- No rankings by playing-style stats (VPIP and so on). They're possible later, but they're easy to fake by logging selectively.
- No prizes, rewards, or anything with monetary value.
- No contact book upload (section 6.2).
- No Game Center integration in v1 (open question 12).

## 3. User stories

1. As a player, I turn on leaderboards, pick a display name, and see nothing change for anyone else until I add friends or join a group.
2. As a player, I add my poker buddy with a friend code he gives me, he accepts, and we each see the other on our Friends board.
3. As a home-game host, I create a private group for my game, invite eight regulars, and we see who played the most hours this month.
4. As a player, I can see that my friend played 22 hours this month, but not how much he won, where he played, or when.
5. As a player who logged a session by mistake, I fix it, and the board updates fairly instead of keeping or losing my rank unfairly.
6. As a player, if someone picks an offensive display name, I can report them, block them, and stop seeing them.
7. As a group admin, I can remove someone who's being abusive, and they can't rejoin with the same invite.
8. As a player who's done with it, I turn leaderboards off and disappear from every board, and my stats stay private as before.
9. As a player who deletes their account, nothing about me remains on any board.
10. As a player who wants peace, I never get leaderboard notifications unless I ask for them.

## 4. Opting in and out

### 4.1 Opting in
- Leaderboards are **off** for every account by default, including existing users after the update.
- Requires a PokerStats account with sync on (1.1). Players who never sign in see a short explanation and a "Turn on Sync" prompt.
- The opt-in screen says plainly what's shared before anything is: "Your display name, hours played, session count and streak will be visible to friends you accept and groups you join. Your money, locations, notes and session details are never shared."
- Steps:
  1. confirm the age statement (section 13);
  2. choose a display name (section 5);
  3. agree to the community rules (short, linked);
  4. done.

  The player then sees an empty Friends board with "Add a friend" and "Create or join a group".

### 4.2 What being opted in creates
- A **leaderboard profile**: the display name, plus the computed board numbers (section 8). This is the only data other users can see.
- The numbers are computed from the player's synced sessions on the server (Max's design). Other users never get access to sessions themselves.

### 4.3 Pausing visibility
"Hide me from boards" keeps friends and groups but shows the player as hidden to others: no name, no numbers. They can still see boards. This is useful during a break.

### 4.4 Opting out
- Settings > Leaderboards > Turn Off. Confirmation: "You'll be removed from all boards and groups, and your friends list will be deleted. Your poker data stays private in your account."
- Effect, immediately on the server:
  - the profile, the display name (released after a hold, section 5.4), friendships, pending invites, and group memberships are deleted;
  - groups the player solely owned are handled per section 7.4;
  - past board positions aren't kept; boards are recomputed without them;
  - reports **against** the player are kept for the moderation retention period (section 11.5), so opting out can't erase evidence of abuse.
- Opting back in starts fresh: new name choice, no friends, no groups.

### 4.5 Deleting the account
- Account deletion (iOS 1.1 sync spec section 10.2, web 1.2 web spec section 12) also deletes everything in 4.4, with the same server-side result.
- Moderation records about the account are kept only as long as section 11.5 allows, with personal data minimized.
- Other users' boards drop the player at the next refresh. No "deleted user" placeholder is shown.

### 4.6 Sync and data interactions
- **Delete All Data, "Delete from all devices"** (1.1 sync spec): the profile stays, but all numbers drop to zero, because the sessions are gone.
- **"Remove from this iPhone"**: no effect on boards. The server data still exists.
- Sessions in **Recently Deleted** don't count. Restoring one counts it again, subject to section 9.
- **Sign out:** no effect on boards. The player stays on boards while signed out, because it's their account, not the device. Open question 2.

## 5. Display names

### 5.1 Rules
- 3 to 20 characters: letters, numbers, spaces, periods, underscores and hyphens. Letters may come from any script. Emoji and invisible or control characters aren't allowed.
- **Unique** across all PokerStats leaderboard profiles, compared without regard to case, accents, or look-alike characters (so "R0han" and "Rohan" count as the same name). Uniqueness is needed so people can confirm they've added the right friend.
- Not shown to strangers. A name is visible only to accepted friends and fellow group members, and to someone the player sends a friend code to.
- The app suggests not using a real full name ("Pick a name your friends will recognize. Avoid your full real name.").

### 5.2 Filtering (guideline 1.2: "a method for filtering objectionable material")
- Every new or changed display name and group name is checked against a profanity and slur list, including common look-alike and spaced-out variants, before it's saved.
- Names impersonating PokerStats ("PokerStats", "Admin", "Support", "Moderator") are reserved.
- Blocked names get a plain message: "That name isn't allowed. Try another." The message doesn't explain which word matched, to make it harder to probe the filter.
- The list lives on the server, so it can be updated without an app release.

### 5.3 Moderation
- Reported names are reviewed (section 11).
- A moderator can **reset** a name. The player is forced to choose a new one on next open, and is shown "Your display name was changed because it broke the community rules."
- Repeat violations can lead to a leaderboard ban (section 11.4).

### 5.4 Changing names
- Allowed **once every 30 days** (resets from moderation don't count).
- Friends and group members see the new name. To limit impersonation games, a small "Previously: OldName" note shows to friends for 7 days after a change. Open question 4.
- A released name can't be claimed by anyone else for 90 days.

## 6. Friends

### 6.1 Adding a friend: recommendation
**Friend codes, shared through the system share sheet or read aloud. No contact upload.**

- Each opted-in player has a **friend code**: short, random, easy to read aloud (for example "KQ7-M4T-9XH"). The code doesn't contain the name, and isn't derived from the email or account ID.
- **Ways to add:**
  - Type a code: Leaderboards > Add Friend > Enter Code.
  - Share your code: "Share My Code" opens the share sheet with a text message ("Add me on PokerStats leaderboards: KQ7-M4T-9XH"). This is consistent with the 1.1 sharing spec: nothing is hosted.
  - Show a QR code of the friend code on screen, for in-person adds at the table. The friend scans it with the in-app scanner; camera permission is asked only when scanning.
- The code can be **regenerated** at any time, which invalidates the old one, for example if it was posted publicly. Pending requests from the old code stay pending.
- **Invite links** (a tappable link that opens the app) need a hosted domain. Following the 1.1 and 1.2 direction of no links, they aren't in v1. They're open question 5, once the 1.2 web domain exists.
- **Contacts:** not in v1. If ever added, it must be opt-in, matched on the device or with hashed identifiers, and never store the contact book. Open question 6.

### 6.2 Requests and accepting
- Entering a code sends a **friend request**. The receiver sees it in Leaderboards > Requests, with the sender's display name, and taps **Accept** or **Decline**. A declined sender isn't told; the request just stays "Pending" on their side until it expires after 30 days.
- Neither side sees the other's numbers until the request is accepted. Friendship is mutual.
- **Rate limits:** at most 20 outgoing requests per day, and at most 10 code-lookup failures per hour. This stops guessing codes to find people.
- **Friend limit:** 200 friends (open question 7).

### 6.3 Removing
- "Remove Friend" from the friend's row. Silent: the other person isn't notified, and both disappear from each other's Friends board.
- Removing doesn't block. They could send a new request, unless blocked (section 11.3).

## 7. Private groups

### 7.1 Creating
- Any opted-in player can create a group: a **group name** (same length, character and filter rules as display names, but not unique) and nothing else. No description, image, or rules text, since those would be more UGC to moderate.
- The creator is the **owner**.
- Limit: each player can own up to 5 groups and be a member of up to 20 (open question 7).

### 7.2 Inviting and joining
- Each group has a **group code**, shared like a friend code (share sheet text, QR, read aloud). Only the owner and admins can see it or regenerate it.
- Owners and admins can also invite friends directly from their friends list.
- Joining by code shows the group name and member count first, then **Join**. Joining by code is immediate unless the group has **"Approve new members"** on (default **on**), in which case an admin approves.
- **Size limit: 50 members** per group (open question 7). A full group shows "This group is full."

### 7.3 Roles

| Role | Can |
|---|---|
| **Owner** (exactly one) | Everything an admin can, plus rename the group, promote or demote admins, transfer ownership, delete the group |
| **Admin** | Approve requests, invite, remove members, view and regenerate the group code |
| **Member** | See the group's boards, invite friends if the owner allows it (default off), leave |

### 7.4 Leaving, removing, deleting
- **Leave:** any member, at any time, silently. Their numbers leave the group's boards immediately.
- **Remove (kick):** admins can remove members, owners can remove admins. A removed member is told: "You were removed from [group name]." A removed member **can't rejoin with the group code** unless an admin re-invites them.
- **Owner leaving or deleting the account:** ownership passes to the longest-serving admin, otherwise to the longest-serving member. If nobody's left, the group is deleted.
- **Delete group:** owner only, with confirmation. Members are told "[group name] was deleted."
- Groups with only one member for 90 days are deleted automatically.

### 7.5 Members within a group
- Members of the same group see each other's display names and group board numbers, even when they aren't friends.
- Being in a group doesn't make people friends. Friend requests between group members use the normal flow.
- **Blocking inside a group** (section 11.3): if A blocks B and both are in the same group, each is hidden from the other on that group's board, and B can't see A's numbers. Admins see a note if a member has been blocked by several members (counts only, not who).

## 8. Metrics and periods

### 8.1 Metrics
Only qualifying sessions count (section 9).

| Board | What it measures | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| **Hours played** | Sum of qualifying session durations in the period (end time minus start time, capped per section 9) | Shown to one decimal ("22.5 h") |
| **Sessions** | Number of qualifying sessions in the period | |
| **Weekly streak** | Consecutive calendar weeks, up to and including the current week, with at least one qualifying session | Shown as "6 weeks". The current week counts as kept until it ends; it's shown as "at risk" only to the player themselves. |

- **Why weekly streaks and not daily:** a daily streak rewards playing poker every day, which pushes toward problem play and conflicts with the gambling-adjacent concerns in section 13. Weekly streaks reward consistency without that pressure. Open question 3.
- **Hand counts** aren't a board metric: not everyone logs hands, and logging is easy to pad.
- All three use only completed sessions, matching how iOS and web stats count sessions (1.2 web spec, section 7). Tournament and sit-and-go sessions count the same as cash (open question 9).

### 8.2 Periods
- **This week**, **This month**, and **All time** for hours and sessions. The streak board shows the current streak, plus each player's own best streak (shown to themselves only).
- Weeks run **Monday to Sunday**. Months are calendar months.
- Past periods: "Last week" and "Last month" final results are kept and viewable until the next period ends. No history beyond that in v1.

### 8.3 Time zones
- Periods are defined in **each player's own time zone**, the one set on their account (taken from the iPhone's zone, and editable in Settings). It's the same idea as the 1.2 web spec's local grouping.
- A session belongs to the period containing its **start time** in the player's time zone.
- On a shared board, each row is the player's own week or month. Boards say "Weeks run Monday to Sunday in each player's time zone." This avoids a session flipping between weeks depending on who's looking.
- A player can change the account time zone at most once every 7 days, so moving it can't be used to double-count a week.

### 8.4 Ranking and ties
- Ranked from highest to lowest. Ties share a rank ("2nd" twice, then 4th), shown with an "=" mark.
- The order within a tie is alphabetical by display name, for display only.
- Players with 0 in a period are listed at the bottom as "No sessions yet", not ranked. Hidden players (section 4.3) aren't listed at all.

### 8.5 Freshness
- Boards update within **15 minutes** of a qualifying session syncing (Max to confirm; real time isn't needed).
- A board shows "Updated 5 min ago".
- A session still active on an iPhone doesn't count until it ends.

## 9. Anti-gaming rules

Boards use self-reported data, so the rules aim to make cheating pointless and visible, not impossible.

### 9.1 What counts as a qualifying session
A completed session counts toward boards only if all of these hold:
1. **Duration** is at least 15 minutes. A session's counted hours are **capped at 16 hours**, and sessions recorded as longer than 24 hours don't count at all.
2. **Recorded live**: the session was started in the app at roughly the time it says it started (its start time is within 1 hour of when it was first created on the device). Sessions entered or moved after the fact don't count. Today the iOS app always starts sessions at the current time and doesn't let users edit start or end times, so normal use always qualifies.
3. **Not overlapping:** if two sessions overlap in time, the overlapping part counts only once for hours. Both still count as sessions only if each has at least 15 minutes that doesn't overlap the other.
4. **Not in the future**, and not started more than 1 hour before the account's leaderboard opt-in date (for the week, month and all-time boards). Earlier history stays private and doesn't count. This stops someone opting in with years of padded entries (open question 8).
5. **Weekly cap:** at most 80 counted hours per player per week. Anything above is ignored for boards.

The exact numbers (15 minutes, 16 hours, 24 hours, 80 hours) are proposals for the owner (open question 8).

### 9.2 Edits and deletions
- Editing money fields, location, stakes or notes doesn't affect boards.
- If an edit ever changes **start or end times** (not possible today; any future editor must follow this rule), the session is re-checked against 9.1. An edit that would increase counted hours for a period that has **already ended** doesn't count. Decreases always apply.
- Deleting a session removes it from boards at the next refresh. Restoring from Recently Deleted counts it again only for periods that haven't ended yet.
- Final results for ended periods (last week, last month) are locked 48 hours after the period ends, so late edits can't rewrite them. All-time keeps updating.

### 9.3 Visible signals, not accusations
- A player whose numbers are capped sees "Some hours weren't counted (limits for leaderboards)" with a link to the rules. Others don't see this.
- Anyone can report implausible numbers (section 11). Moderators can **exclude a player from all boards** for a period or permanently, without touching their private data.
- Multiple accounts: one leaderboard profile per account. A moderation ban applies to the account. Detecting duplicate accounts is out of scope for v1.

## 10. What other users can and can't see

| Visible to friends and fellow group members | Never visible to other users |
|---|---|
| Display name | Money of any kind: results, buy-ins, cash-outs, tips, hourly rates, BB |
| Hours played, sessions and current weekly streak, for the periods in section 8 | Location or casino |
| Rank on each board | Notes, mental state, hands and stats |
| Group membership: who else is in a shared group | Session dates and times, including when someone is playing **right now** |
| | Stakes and game types |
| | Seat names from table setups |
| | Email, sign-in provider, account ID, time zone, friend code (unless the player shares it) |
| | Their friends list, or groups not shared with the viewer |
| | Whether they've blocked, reported or removed anyone |

- **Not showing "playing now"** is deliberate. A live indicator, or a board that jumps during a session, would reveal when and possibly where someone is playing. Boards change only after sessions end (8.5).
- These limits match the 1.1 sharing defaults (no money, location, dates, stakes or seat names) and the 1.2 web rule of no public pages.
- Anything on the "never" list may only appear on a board after a future, explicit owner decision **and** a per-user opt-in. Money boards are out of scope for v1 entirely.

## 11. Report, block, and remove (guideline 1.2)

### 11.1 What guideline 1.2 minimally requires, and the plan for each

| Requirement | 1.3 plan |
|---|---|
| A method for filtering objectionable material | The name filter on display names and group names (5.2). There's no other free text. |
| A mechanism to report offensive content, with timely responses | Report a user or a group (11.2). Reviewed within 24 hours (11.5). |
| The ability to block abusive users | Block (11.3). |
| Published contact information | A support email shown in Settings > Leaderboards > Help, on the community rules page, in the privacy policy, and on the App Store listing. |

Keeping the UGC surface to **two short names** (display name and group name), with no chat, bios or images, is what makes solo-developer moderation realistic.

### 11.2 Reporting
- **"Report"** is on every player row, in a group's member list, and on a group invite screen (to report a group name).
- Reasons:
  - offensive display name;
  - offensive group name;
  - harassment;
  - impersonation;
  - impossible numbers or cheating;
  - other, with up to 200 characters of optional details. That text is seen only by moderators.
- After reporting: "Thanks. We'll review this within 24 hours." with an offer to block the person as well.
- Reports are anonymous to the reported user.
- A player can't report the same target for the same reason more than once a day.

### 11.3 Blocking
- **"Block"** is on any player row, member list entry, or friend request.
- Blocking takes effect immediately:
  - the friendship is removed;
  - pending requests in either direction are cancelled;
  - the blocked person can't send new friend requests, and their code lookups for the blocker fail as "Code not found";
  - in shared groups, each is hidden from the other's view of the board (7.5).
- The blocked person isn't told.
- Settings > Leaderboards > Blocked lists blocks with "Unblock".

### 11.4 Removing (moderator actions)
Possible actions:
- reset a display name;
- rename or delete a group;
- remove a player from a group;
- exclude a player from boards (temporary or permanent);
- ban the account from leaderboards, which is the same as forced opt-out plus a block on opting back in.

The affected player is told what happened and why, in one sentence, with the support email for appeals.

### 11.5 Who reviews, and the minimum tooling
- **Reviewer:** the owner (or a delegate the owner names) in 1.3. Open question 1.
- **Target:** first review within 24 hours of a report, every day including weekends. Section 1.2 expects timely responses.
- **Minimum tooling** (Max designs it):
  - a private, admin-only report queue, showing the reported name, reason, details, reporter count and the target's board numbers;
  - one-click actions from 11.4;
  - an email alert to the reviewer for each new report;
  - an action log.

  No poker data beyond board numbers is shown to moderators.
- **Automatic safety net:** if a display name or group name is reported by 3 or more different players, it's hidden (shown as "Player" or "Group") until reviewed.
- **Retention:** reports and actions are kept for 1 year, then deleted. Reports about a deleted account keep only the content reported and the action taken.

## 12. Notifications

- **All leaderboard notifications are off by default.** The app asks for notification permission only when the player turns one on, never during opt-in.
- Available toggles (Settings > Leaderboards > Notifications):
  - **Friend requests and group invites:** "Jordan wants to add you as a friend."
  - **Weekly summary**, Monday morning in the player's time zone: "Last week: 3rd in Home Game hours." One per week at most.
- **Never sent:** "You were passed by X", streak-at-risk nudges, "Your friends are playing now", or anything that encourages playing more or more often. Moderation notices appear in the app, not as pushes.
- The web shows requests and invites in the page. There are no web push notifications in v1.

## 13. Minors and age rating

- PokerStats is a tracker for real-world poker. It has no real-money gaming. Adding social comparison of gambling activity may still change how App Review and the age rating questionnaire treat the app.
- **Age rating:** re-answer the questionnaire for 1.3. Expect to answer for user-generated content and for any gambling-related themes. Plan for an **18+ / 17+** rating, the highest available under the current rating system [verify the current categories and the app's existing rating in App Store Connect].
- **In-app age gate:** opt-in requires confirming "I'm 18 or older, or the legal age to gamble where I live." Users who say no can't turn on leaderboards. The rest of the app is unaffected.
- **Responsible play:** the rules page links to a problem gambling resource, and the metric choices (weekly streaks, hour caps, no money boards, no "play more" notifications) are part of the responsible design. Section 1.2 doesn't cover this, but App Review may look at it under 5.3 and 1.1.
- **App Review notes:** explain that leaderboards are friends and private groups only, opt-in, ranked by time and session counts, never money, with filtering, reporting, blocking and a contact email. Provide a demo account already in a test group with a second demo friend, so the reviewer can see reporting and blocking.

## 15. Platforms

- **iOS:** everything in this spec.
- **Web (1.2 app):** boards, friends and groups, requests, report and block, opt-in and opt-out, and name changes. QR scanning is iOS-only; the web shows the QR and the code. This is consistent with the 1.2 web spec:

## 16. Abuse and harassment edge cases

| Case | Handling |
|---|---|
| Offensive or slur display name that gets past the filter | Report, auto-hide at 3 reports, moderator reset, filter list updated. |
| Impersonation (a name like a friend's, or a pro player's) | Uniqueness and look-alike rules, the "Previously" note after changes, an impersonation report reason, and a moderator reset. |
| Ex-partner or stalker trying to track when someone plays | No live status, no session times, boards update only after sessions end, weekly granularity for streaks, block hides everything, code lookups fail for blocked users. Remaining risk: a friend can infer "played this week". The player can hide themselves (4.3) or remove the friend. |
| Spamming friend requests | 20 per day, code regeneration, blocking, and repeated declines (5 from different people in a day) pause the sender's requests for 24 hours. |
| Guessing friend codes to find people | Random codes, failure rate limit, codes don't reveal the name until a request is sent. The receiver must accept before any numbers are visible. |
| Hostile group admin (kicking people to humiliate them, offensive group name) | Members can leave silently. Group name reports go to moderators, who can rename or delete. A removed member's view only says they were removed. |
| Group used to coordinate harassment of a member | The member can leave and block. A group report triggers review; moderators can delete the group. |
| Cheating by fake hours | Section 9 caps and rules, reporting, board exclusion. |
| Retaliatory or mass false reporting | One report per reason per day per reporter, auto-hide only hides a name (no penalty), and the moderator decides. Abusive reporters can be banned. |
| Minor joins despite the age gate | Report reason "other". The moderator can ban from leaderboards. The rest of the app stays usable. |
| Account deleted mid-dispute | Reports keep the reported content and action, per 11.5. |
| Shared group with someone you blocked | Mutually hidden on that board; neither sees the other's numbers. |

## 17. Acceptance criteria (testable)

1. A new or existing account has leaderboards **off**. No profile exists, and no other user can find it by any means, including the correct display name.
2. Opt-in requires the age confirmation, a display name passing the rules, and accepting the community rules, and shows the "what's shared" text before enabling.
3. Display names: 2 or 21 characters, emoji, control characters, filtered words and look-alike variants (e.g. "R0han" when "Rohan" exists) are refused with the generic message. A second change within 30 days is refused.
4. Friend codes: entering a valid code sends a request, and nothing is visible to either side until accepted. 11 wrong codes in an hour are rate-limited. A regenerated code invalidates the old one.
5. Friend requests: accept makes both appear on each other's Friends board. Decline isn't revealed to the sender. Requests expire after 30 days.
6. Groups: owner, admin and member permissions behave per 7.3. A 51st member is refused. A removed member can't rejoin with the code. Ownership transfers as specified when the owner leaves.
7. Metrics: for a fixture account, hours, sessions and weekly streak match hand-calculated values for this week, this month and all time, in the player's time zone. Weeks run Monday to Sunday.
8. Ties share a rank and are ordered by display name. Zero-activity players are unranked at the bottom. Hidden players don't appear.
9. Anti-gaming:
   - a 10-minute session doesn't count;
   - a 20-hour session counts as 16 hours;
   - a 25-hour session doesn't count;
   - two overlapping sessions count their overlap once;
   - a session whose start is more than 1 hour before its creation doesn't count;
   - sessions before the opt-in date don't count;
   - more than 80 hours in a week is capped.
10. Edits and deletes: deleting a session removes it from boards within 15 minutes. Ended periods lock 48 hours after the end. Increases to ended periods are ignored, and decreases apply.
11. **Visibility:** through every app screen, web page and API response available to another user, no money, location, notes, stakes, game type, session time, seat name, email, provider or time zone of another player is ever returned. Max to provide an automated test against RLS and API responses.
12. Report: available on every player row, member entry and group invite. Submitting shows the confirmation, creates a queue item and emails the reviewer. Three distinct reports auto-hide the name.
13. Block: takes effect immediately as in 11.3, including in shared groups. The blocked user's lookups of the blocker's code fail as not found. Unblock restores the ability to send requests, not the old friendship.
14. Moderator actions in 11.4 work from the admin tool and notify the affected player in the app.
15. The support contact is visible in Settings > Leaderboards > Help, the community rules, the privacy policy and the App Store listing.
16. Opt-out removes the profile, friendships, memberships and pending requests immediately, and boards recompute without the player within 15 minutes. Account deletion does the same plus everything in the sync spec.
17. Notifications: none are sent by default, permission isn't requested at opt-in, and only the two toggles in section 12 exist.
18. Boards never change during an active session on any viewer's screen, and no "playing now" state exists.
19. The age rating questionnaire, privacy policy and App Privacy label are updated before submission, and App Review notes plus a demo account are prepared.
20. Web pages for leaderboards meet WCAG 2.2 AA and are only reachable while signed in.
