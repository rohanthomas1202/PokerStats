## 1. Problem

After 1.1, a signed-in player's sessions and hands sync to Supabase, but the only way to look at them is a phone screen. Reviewing your game is a desk activity:
- Trends charts, the session list grouped by month, and Leak Finder are cramped on a phone and easier to study on a large screen.
- Players want to look at their results on a laptop between sessions, without picking up the phone.
- Players without an iPhone handy (or who want to show a coach on a shared screen) have no way in.

Google Sign-In is also missing. Many players expect it on the web, and the 1.0 iOS app offers only Sign in with Apple.

---

## 2. Who it's for

- **Reviewing at a desk**, on a laptop or desktop browser, and sometimes on a tablet or a phone browser.
- The same players who log on iOS. In 1.2 the web is a **companion** to the iPhone app, not a replacement.
- **Not** for logging at the table. Live session and hand logging stay on iOS, where the 3-second logging flow, the Live Activity and offline queueing live.

---

## 3. Goals and non-goals

### Goals
1. A signed-in player sees the same dashboard, stats, trends and session history on the web as on their iPhone, with **identical numbers**.
2. Sign in with Apple and Google Sign-In both work on the web. Google also comes to iOS in 1.2. One person ends up with one account.
3. Private by default: nothing is visible to anyone but the account owner, with no public pages.
4. Fast and accessible (WCAG 2.2 AA) on desktop, tablet and mobile browsers.
5. A player can delete their account from the web, with the same result as on iOS.

### Non-goals for 1.2
- **Live session logging and hand logging on the web.** No "Start Session", no hand logger, no rebuys or check-ins during a session.
- Public profiles, share links, public stats pages, or leaderboards (1.3).
- Image sharing from the web (1.1 sharing is iOS-only).
- An Android app, or installing the web app on a home screen as a substitute app (an installable PWA is open question 9).
- Offline use of the web app. It needs a connection; iOS is the offline device.
- Coaching or multi-user access to someone else's data.
- Importing data from other trackers.
- Tournament features (#8). The web must display tournament sessions sensibly if they exist (section 6.6), nothing more.
- Notifications or emails, beyond any security emails the auth provider sends.

---

## 4. User stories

1. As a player at my desk, I sign in on the web with Apple or Google and see my dashboard with the same total P/L, hourly rate and VPIP as my iPhone.
2. As a player, I filter Trends to the last 90 days on a big screen and study my bankroll chart and day-of-week results.
3. As a player, I browse my session history by month, search by location or stakes, and open a session to see its stats, position breakdown and hand log.
4. As a player, I fix a typo in a session's location or cash-out on the web, and my iPhone shows the fix after it syncs (if editing is approved; section 5.2).
5. As a player who signed up with Apple on iOS, I sign in on the web with Apple and land in the same account. If I sign in with Google using the same email, I'm not silently given a second, empty account.
6. As a player who hasn't synced yet, the web tells me how to get my data there instead of showing zeros.
7. As a privacy-minded player, I know no one else can see my data, the site doesn't track me, and closing a public computer's browser doesn't leave me signed in for long.
8. As a player leaving PokerStats, I delete my account from the web and everything is removed, including on my iPhone.
9. As a player using a screen reader or keyboard only, I can use every page.
10. As a player, I download my data from the web (if export is in 1.2; section 13).

## 5. v1 scope

### 5.1 Pages

The pages mirror the iOS tabs so players don't have to learn anything new.

| Page | Matches iOS | v1 content |
|---|---|---|
| **Dashboard** | `DashboardView` | Total P/L, hourly rate, hours, sessions. Playing tendencies (play style label, VPIP/PFR chart). Stat cards: VPIP, PFR, C-bet, WTSD, Fold to 3-bet, W$SD, hands. Fold frequency (preflop fold, fold to 3-bet). Position breakdown. Mental insights (calm vs tilted hourly rate, when there's enough data). Leak Finder summary. Recent sessions (last 5 completed). Shows "Session in progress on your iPhone" when one is active (section 6.5). |
| **Stats help** | `StatHelpView`, `StatDefinition` | The same definitions and "good range" explanations, shown on hover, focus or tap. The wording comes from one shared source (section 7). |
| **Trends** | `TrendsView` | Time period: All Time, 30d, 90d, 6mo (same as iOS). Bankroll chart, VPIP/PFR trend, day-of-week results, P/L distribution. Each chart also has a data table (section 9). |
| **Leak Finder** | `LeakFinderView` | **In v1 if cheap** (recommended in): it's a pure calculation over the same stats, with a profile picker (6-Max, Full Ring) and the 20-hand minimum. If it threatens the date, it ships as a dashboard summary only, with the full page in 1.2.x. |
| **History** | `SessionHistoryView` | Completed sessions grouped by month, with the period totals ("12 sessions, 41h played"), and search by location or stakes (same as iOS). Sorting by date, newest first. |
| **Session detail** | `SessionDetailView` | Header (stakes, game type, date, duration, location). Financials (buy-in, rebuys, add-ons, cash-out, tips, $/hr, ROI). Session stats and play style. Mental state. Position breakdown. Session insights. Hand log. Notes. |
| **Settings** | `SettingsView` (subset) | Account (signed-in email and providers), linked sign-in methods (section 8.3), defaults (game type, stakes, buy-in, currency; these are synced settings), Delete Account, privacy policy link, sign out, and export if in scope. |

Planned iOS features appear on the web once they exist in synced data: tips and add-ons (#27) in financials, and stack checks with the P/L chart (#30) in session detail. These are listed so the web isn't designed in a way that blocks them; they ship when the iOS features ship.

### 5.2 Editing: recommendation

**Recommendation: read-only in the first 1.2 release, with limited editing of completed sessions as the first follow-up (1.2.1).**

Why:
- Read-only removes all web-side conflict cases on day one. The web becomes a pure consumer of the 1.1 sync data, which de-risks launch.
- The 1.1 sync rules (field-level last edit wins, delete wins, Recently Deleted) already handle edits from any client, so adding web editing later requires no new rules.
- The iOS app's own editing is narrow anyway: session detail edits buy-in, rebuys, cash-out, location, stakes and notes (`SessionDetailViewModel`).

**Planned 1.2.1 editing scope** (for the owner to confirm now, so the design allows it):
- Edit a completed session's buy-in, rebuys, add-ons, cash-out, tips, location, stakes, game type and notes. The same fields as iOS, plus the ones #27 adds.
- Delete a session (goes to Recently Deleted for 30 days, per the 1.1 sync spec).
- Restore from Recently Deleted.
- Not editable on the web: hands, start and end times, table setups, active sessions.
- Edits follow the 1.1 sync conflict rules. If the session changes on another device while the web form is open, the web shows "Updated on another device" and reloads the field values before saving, so the player never unknowingly overwrites a newer change.

Open question 1 asks the owner to choose between this recommendation and full editing at launch.

### 5.3 Explicitly not in v1
- Starting, running, or ending a session.
- Logging, editing, or deleting hands.
- Table setup and auto-position.
- Mental check-ins during a session.
- Anything that needs the Live Activity or widgets.
- Data seeding (the DEBUG "Seed test data" never appears on the web).

## 6. Behavior details

### 6.1 Data freshness
- The web shows the account's current synced data at page load.
- It updates without a reload when the data changes on another device while the page is open, or at least on focus. Live updates vs refresh-on-focus is open question M3 for Max.
- A "Last updated" time is visible in Settings, and on Dashboard as a small caption.

### 6.2 Units and formatting
- Money uses the account's currency setting. iOS formats in USD today regardless of the setting. Web and iOS must format the same way, so fixing currency is a shared change, not a web-only one.
- Dates and times show in the **browser's time zone**, with the zone named where a time is shown ("Oct 17, 9:42 PM CDT"). Sessions are grouped by month and by day of week in the browser's zone. That matches iOS, which uses the phone's zone. A player traveling may see a session move across midnight, which is acceptable and should be documented.
- Percentages and rounding match iOS exactly (section 7).

### 6.3 Search and filters
- History search matches the iOS behavior (location or stakes, case-insensitive).
- Trends periods are rolling windows from now, like iOS.

### 6.4 Navigation
- Every page has its own address (dashboard, trends, history, a session) so the browser's back button, bookmarks and new tabs work.
- These addresses are only usable by the signed-in owner. Anyone else, signed in or not, gets the sign-in page or "This page isn't available." Session addresses must not expose anything guessable or meaningful (no dates, locations or amounts).

### 6.5 Active sessions
- A session in progress on an iPhone appears on the Dashboard as "Session in progress on your iPhone", with stakes, start time, hands so far and invested, updating as it syncs.
- It's read-only, it's excluded from money stats (as on iOS, where only completed sessions count), and it has no "end" or "log" action.
- Active sessions don't appear in History until completed (as on iOS).

### 6.6 Tournament and sit-and-go sessions
- The game type already allows Tournament and Sit and Go. The web shows them with the same game type label as iOS and includes them in the same totals iOS does.
- When #8 adds tournament fields, the web adds them. Until then nothing special is done.

## 7. Stats parity with iOS

### 7.1 Requirement
For the same account data, every number on the web equals the number on iOS, to the displayed precision. That covers:
- money stats: total P/L, hourly rate, hours, sessions, average per session, ROI;
- player stats: VPIP, PFR, C-bet, WTSD, W$SD, Fold to 3-bet;
- per-position stats;
- play style classification;
- Trends series (bankroll, VPIP/PFR, day of week, P/L distribution buckets);
- mental-state correlation;
- Leak Finder ratings and the 20-hand minimum.

The **definitions** match too: what counts as a hand, a VPIP or a c-bet opportunity, which sessions count (completed only for money), how durations are measured, and how rounding works.

### 7.2 How a mismatch is prevented
1. **One written definition per stat**, kept in the repo and referenced by both apps. Today the definitions only exist as code in `StatCalculator`, `TrendCalculator`, `LeakFinder` and the stat help text. The help text on both platforms comes from this one source.
2. **Shared reference datasets** (golden fixtures): a set of anonymized accounts covering edge cases, each with expected values for every stat. Edge cases include:
   - no hands, one hand;
   - sessions under 36 seconds (hourly rate is nil below 0.01 hours);
   - abandoned sessions (cash-out 0);
   - mixed stakes;
   - every preflop and postflop result;
   - sessions crossing midnight and DST changes;
   - tips and add-ons;
   - tournaments.
3. **Both apps' tests run the fixtures in CI**, and a change to a stat definition must update the definition, the fixtures, and both apps in the same change.
4. **Where stats are calculated is Max's decision** (1.1 sync spec open question 2), but the requirement doesn't change with the choice:
   - computed once on the server for both apps;
   - computed in each app from the shared definitions;
   - a mix.
   If iOS keeps computing on the device (it must work offline), the fixtures are what keeps the two in line.

### 7.3 How a mismatch is detected
- **In CI:** the fixture tests above fail the build.
- **In production, without user tracking:** a test account (owned by the team, seeded with a fixture dataset) is checked on a schedule. Its stats are compared between the web and the iOS calculation, and any difference alerts the team. No real user's data is used for this.
- **From users:** "Numbers don't match my iPhone?" in the web Settings links to help text explaining sync timing ("Your iPhone may have changes that haven't synced yet") and how to contact support.
- **Version gate:** if a stat definition changes, both apps must ship it together. If an older iOS version would show different numbers, release notes say so, and the old app shows the sync spec's "Update PokerStats" prompt if the data format changed.

## 8. Sign-in and account linking

### 8.1 Providers
- **Sign in with Apple** on the web (Apple's web flow).
- **Google Sign-In** on the web, and added to iOS in the same 1.2 release (#2, #16).
- No email and password, no magic links in 1.2 (open question 4).
- The web sign-in page shows both buttons with equal prominence, following each provider's branding rules.

### 8.2 One person, one account
These rules apply on web and iOS alike.

| Situation | Behavior |
|---|---|
| Apple account already used on iOS, now on the web | Same PokerStats account. Data is there. |
| New user signs in with Google first | A new account is created. |
| Google sign-in where the Google email **equals the email on an existing account** (for example an Apple account that shared its real email) | **No silent merge and no second account.** The player is told: "An account with this email already exists. Sign in with Apple to continue, then add Google in Settings." Automatic linking by email is open question 2. |
| Apple "Hide My Email" user (relay address) signs in with Google | The emails differ, so this is treated as a new person: a new, empty account. The sign-in page and empty dashboard both point to "Already use PokerStats on iPhone? Sign in with Apple instead." |
| Signed in, adding a second provider in Settings ("Link Google" or "Link Apple") | Works whether the emails match or not, because the player has proved they own both. Afterwards either provider opens the same account. |
| Linking a provider that's already attached to **another** PokerStats account that has data | Refused in 1.2: "This Google account is already used by another PokerStats account." Merging two accounts' data is out of scope (1.1 sync spec non-goal). Open question 3. |
| Unlinking | Allowed only when another sign-in method stays linked. The last one can't be removed; the player deletes the account instead. |

### 8.3 Settings: sign-in methods
- Lists linked providers with the email each one reported (relay addresses shown as "Hidden by Apple").
- "Link Google" or "Link Apple" for the missing one, and "Unlink" when allowed.
- Linking and unlinking work the same in the iOS Settings screen in 1.2.

### 8.4 Constraint for Max
Apple sign-in on the web uses a different Apple client identifier (a Services ID) than the iOS app (the bundle ID).

Today's `delete-account` function is built around a single configured Apple client ID:
- it exchanges the authorization code with that client ID;
- since #42 (PR #96), it rejects an Apple token whose audience isn't that client ID, with `apple_token_invalid`.

So an Apple re-authentication done on the web would currently fail. Account deletion from the web (section 12) must still revoke the Apple token correctly. See question M5.

## 9. Responsive layouts and accessibility

### 9.1 Layouts

| Width | Layout |
|---|---|
| **Desktop** (wide) | Left navigation (Dashboard, Trends, Leak Finder, History, Settings). Dashboard in a multi-column grid. History as a list with the selected session's detail beside it. |
| **Tablet** (medium) | Collapsible navigation. Two-column dashboard. History and detail as separate pages. |
| **Mobile web** (narrow) | Bottom or top navigation. Single column, the same order as the iOS app. Charts fit the width and scroll horizontally only when the data requires it. |

- All content is reachable at 320 CSS px wide, and at 400 percent zoom, without loss of content or function (WCAG reflow).
- Both portrait and landscape work on tablets and phones.
- Theme: dark by default to match iOS. A light theme that follows the system setting is open question 8.

### 9.2 Accessibility to WCAG 2.2 AA
All pages meet WCAG 2.2 Level AA. Specifically:
- **Keyboard:** every action works with the keyboard alone, the focus order is logical, focus is always visible and never hidden behind sticky headers (2.4.11 Focus Not Obscured), and there are no keyboard traps.
- **Screen readers:** a semantic structure (landmarks, headings, lists, tables), labels on all controls, and stat values read with their names and units ("VPIP, 24.3 percent").
- **Charts:** each chart has a text summary ("Bankroll went from $0 to $1,240 over 90 days") and a "Show as table" option with the same data.
- **Color:** not the only signal. Profit and loss always carry a + or minus sign. Play style and Leak Finder ratings have text labels. Contrast is at least 4.5:1 for text and 3:1 for UI components and chart lines in every theme. Today's iOS secondary text color (white at 55 percent opacity on near-black) must be checked.
- **Target size:** interactive targets are at least 24 by 24 CSS px (2.5.8).
- **Text:** resizes to 200 percent without breaking, and honors browser text spacing overrides.
- **Motion:** respects reduced-motion preferences, with no auto-playing animation.
- **Sign-in:** no cognitive tests. Apple and Google flows satisfy accessible authentication (3.3.8). Sign-in errors are announced.
- **Time limits:** before the session times out, there's a warning with an option to stay signed in (section 11), unless the limit exists for security and is announced.
- **Help:** the help link is in the same place on every page (3.2.6 Consistent Help).
- **Testing:** automated checks in CI on every page, plus a manual pass with VoiceOver (Safari), NVDA (Firefox or Chrome) and keyboard only before launch.

## 10. Empty and edge states

| State | What the player sees |
|---|---|
| Signed in, account has **no data at all**, never used the iPhone app | "No sessions yet. PokerStats sessions are logged in the iPhone app at the table. [Get the app] Your stats will appear here once you've played." |
| Signed in, has the iPhone app but **sync isn't on or hasn't run** (for example an Apple account with only 1.0 backups) | "Your sessions haven't synced yet. On your iPhone, update PokerStats and open Settings > Sync. They'll appear here automatically." |
| Signed in with a **different provider** than the iPhone account (likely a separate empty account) | Adds: "Use a different sign-in on your iPhone? Sign out and sign in with Apple." |
| First sync **in progress** from the iPhone | "Your iPhone is still uploading sessions (840 of 1,240 hands). This page updates automatically." |
| Sessions but **fewer than 20 hands** | Stats show "--" with "Log at least 20 hands to see this stat", matching the iOS hand minimums. Leak Finder explains its minimum. |
| Sessions but **no hands** (money-only users) | Money stats and charts show. Hand-based sections are replaced by a short explanation instead of empty cards. |
| A Trends **period with no sessions** | "No sessions in the last 30 days." The period picker stays usable. |
| History **search with no matches** | "No sessions match 'Bellagio'." with a clear-search button. |
| **Session not found** (deleted elsewhere, or not the player's) | "This session isn't available. It may have been deleted on another device." [Back to History]. Same message for not-found and not-yours, so it reveals nothing. |
| **Can't reach the server** | "Can't load your data right now. Check your connection and try again." [Try again]. Nothing is shown as zero. |
| **Account deleted elsewhere** | Signed out, with "This PokerStats account was deleted." |

## 11. Session timeout and security

- **Sign-in session length:** a signed-in browser stays signed in for at most **30 days** of total time, and is signed out after **7 days of inactivity**. Proposals; open question 5.
- A **"This is a shared or public computer"** option on the sign-in page shortens this to the browser session, so closing the browser signs out.
- An **expiry warning** two minutes before an inactivity sign-out, if a short limit is chosen, with "Stay signed in".
- **Sign-out** clears the session from the browser. "Sign out of all devices" in Settings ends every web session for the account. Whether it also signs out the iPhone is open question 6.
- **Re-authentication** (signing in again with Apple or Google) is required for Delete Account, and for linking or unlinking a sign-in method.
- **Expired session** on the web: the player is returned to sign-in with "You've been signed out. Sign in again to continue," and lands back on the page they were on.
- **Requirements for Max:**
  - All data access goes through Supabase auth and row-level security, so a user can only ever read or write their own rows. The web must never hold credentials that bypass that for user requests.
  - HTTPS only, with HSTS.
  - Tokens in storage that page scripts can't read where the chosen stack allows it.
  - Protection against cross-site request forgery and clickjacking.
  - A strict content security policy.
  - No third-party scripts on signed-in pages (section 14).
- **Abuse:** sign-in attempts are rate-limited by the auth provider. The web adds no extra password or account recovery surface, since there are no passwords.

## 12. Account deletion from the web

- **Settings > Delete Account**, behind a confirmation that names what will be deleted: "This permanently deletes your PokerStats account and all your sessions, hands and settings on our servers, on the web, and on every iPhone signed in to this account. This can't be undone."
- The player re-authenticates with a linked provider. For Apple-linked accounts this provides a fresh Apple authorization so the Apple token can be revoked (constraint 8.4).
- **Server result is identical to iOS Delete Account** (1.1 sync spec, section 10.2):
  - synced data, Recently Deleted, old backups, profile and auth user are deleted;
  - the Apple token is revoked if Apple is linked;
  - the Google link is removed, and revoked if the design holds a Google token (M5).
- The web signs out and shows: "Your account has been deleted." Signed-in iPhones handle it as in the sync spec (sign out, remove local data, show a notice).
- **Failure:** if deletion fails or Apple re-authentication is needed, nothing is deleted and the player sees a clear retry message, mirroring today's coded failures (`apple_reauth_required`, `apple_token_invalid`, `apple_account_mismatch`).
- **Export before deleting:** if export is available (section 13), the confirmation offers "Download your data first".
- App Review 5.1.1(v) covers the iOS app. The web matching it keeps the promise consistent, and the privacy policy describes both.

## 13. Data export (#9)

- **Recommendation:** put export on the web, in **Settings > Your data > Download your data**. It's a natural fit for a desk, and it's the obvious place to get a file onto a computer.
- **Formats:** CSV (one file each for sessions and hands, opening cleanly in spreadsheets) and JSON (complete, for portability), downloaded as one archive.
- **Contents:** everything synced for the account, including table setups with seat names. It's the player's own copy, so the 1.1 sharing privacy limits don't apply. The file is made on request and never emailed or hosted.
- **Timing:** export in 1.2 if Max confirms it's small. Otherwise 1.2.1. Open question 7.
- **iOS:** an iOS export (#9) can reuse the same format later, but isn't required for 1.2.

## 14. Privacy

1. **Private by default.** All data is visible only to its owner. There are no public profile pages, share pages, or public stats in 1.2, and nothing can be found by search engines. Signed-in pages are marked not to be indexed, and only the sign-in and marketing pages are public.
2. **Cookies:** only **strictly necessary** ones (sign-in session, security, the remembered theme). No advertising or cross-site tracking cookies. Because of that, no cookie consent banner should be needed [verify with the privacy policy owner for the EU and UK].
3. **Analytics:** none on signed-in pages, consistent with the iOS app and today's privacy policy ("No advertising SDKs, analytics frameworks, or tracking tools"). Two options need an owner decision (open question 10):
   - none at all;
   - privacy-respecting, cookieless, aggregate page counts on **public** pages only.
   Session replay, heatmaps, third-party fonts or scripts loaded from tracking networks, and advertising pixels are never used.
4. **Error reporting:** if Max adds crash or error reporting, it must not capture poker data, emails or page contents, and it goes in the privacy policy (M7).
5. **Privacy policy update** (#54, #72), before launch, covering:
   - the web app;
   - Google as a sign-in provider and what Google shares (name, email);
   - account linking;
   - cookies used;
   - the hosting provider and where data is stored;
   - the session lengths;
   - export;
   - deletion from the web.
   It must be linked from the web footer and the sign-in page.
6. **Data minimization:** the web asks the providers only for the minimum scopes (identity and email). It doesn't request Google contacts, profile photo or other data.

## 16. Acceptance criteria (testable)

1. **Parity:** for every shared reference dataset, every stat on Dashboard, Trends, Leak Finder, History totals and Session detail equals the expected value and the iOS value, at displayed precision.
2. **Parity across a sync:** a session ended on the iPhone appears on an open web Dashboard and changes the totals within 60 seconds, without a manual reload if live updates are chosen, or on focus otherwise (M3).
3. **No logging:** the web has no way to start, end or log a session or hand.
4. **Read-only v1** (if approved): no edit or delete controls exist, and the web makes no data-changing requests besides sign-in, settings that are explicitly in scope, linking, export and Delete Account.
5. **Apple sign-in** on the web opens the same account (and data) as the iPhone app for the same Apple ID.
6. **Google sign-in, new email:** creates a new account with the "Already use PokerStats on iPhone?" hint on the empty dashboard.
7. **Google sign-in, email matching an existing account:** no account is created, nothing is merged, and the "Sign in with Apple, then add Google" message is shown.
8. **Linking:** after linking Google in Settings, signing in with Google opens the same account on web and iOS. Unlinking the last method is impossible. Linking a provider owned by another account with data is refused with the stated message.
9. **Privacy isolation:** user B can't load user A's data by any page address or request. The not-found and not-yours messages are identical.
10. **Signed-in pages** send no requests to third-party analytics, ad or tracking domains (network inspection), set only strictly necessary cookies, and are marked not to be indexed.
11. **Responsive:** every page works at 320 px, 768 px and 1440 px wide and at 400 percent zoom, without horizontal scrolling except inside charts or tables.
12. **Accessibility:** automated checks show no WCAG 2.2 AA violations on any page. A manual keyboard-only and screen reader pass completes every user story in section 4. Every chart has a text summary and a table view.
13. **Empty states:** each state in section 10 shows the stated copy, and no page shows misleading zeros while data is loading or failed.
14. **Session security:**
    - inactivity and absolute timeouts behave as decided;
    - "shared or public computer" signs out when the browser closes;
    - an expired session returns to sign-in and then to the original page;
    - Delete Account and linking require re-authentication.
15. **Delete Account from the web** gives the same server-side result as iOS Delete Account, including Apple token revocation for Apple-linked accounts. A signed-in iPhone then shows the "account was deleted" notice and removes the data.
16. **Export** (if in 1.2) downloads CSV and JSON files whose session and hand counts and money totals equal the app's.
17. **Performance:** the targets in section 15 are met on the 2,000-session, 100,000-hand test account.
18. **Time zones:** month grouping and day-of-week charts use the browser's time zone, and the zone is labeled where times are shown.
19. **Privacy policy** is updated and linked from the sign-in page and footer before launch.

## 17. Open questions

### For the owner
1. **Editing:** read-only at launch with limited editing in 1.2.1 (recommended), or editing of completed sessions at launch?
2. **Linking by email:** never link automatically, as proposed, or link automatically when Apple and Google report the same verified email?
3. **Two existing accounts:** refuse linking when both accounts have data (proposed), or offer an account merge later (1.3 or beyond)?
4. **Other sign-in methods:** Apple and Google only in 1.2, or also email magic links for people without either?
5. **Session lengths:** 30 days absolute and 7 days inactive, plus the shared-computer option, or something stricter?
6. **Sign out everywhere:** should it sign out the iPhone apps too, or only web sessions?
7. **Export (#9):** in 1.2 on the web if small (recommended), or 1.2.1? CSV and JSON both?
8. **Theme:** dark only to match iOS, or dark plus a light theme that follows the system?
9. **Installable web app:** allow home-screen install on phones in 1.2, or not yet?
10. **Analytics:** none at all, or cookieless aggregate counts on public pages only?
11. **Leak Finder:** full page in 1.2 even if it adds time, or a dashboard summary first?
12. **Public marketing page:** should the web root be a public marketing page with sign-in, or go straight to sign-in?
13. **Domain:** what's the web address?

### For Max (technical design)
- **M1. Stack and hosting:**
  - choose the framework and hosting;
  - it must work with Supabase auth (Apple and Google) and row-level security;
  - it must deploy from `web/` in this repo with CI;
  - it should support preview deployments for review.
- **M2. Data access:**
  - how the web reads synced data under RLS;
  - pagination for History and hand logs;
  - how the dashboard avoids downloading every hand (server-side aggregates or views).
- **M3. Freshness:** live updates (subscriptions) or refresh on focus and interval.
- **M4. Stats location:** server, each app from shared definitions, or a mix (1.1 sync open question 2), and how the golden fixtures run in both CI pipelines.
- **M5. Apple and Google tokens:**
  - how Apple revocation works when the account was created or re-authenticated on the web with a Services ID, given today's single Apple client ID in `delete-account`;
  - whether any Google token is stored and must be revoked on deletion.
- **M6. Session handling:** token storage, refresh and timeout mechanics within Supabase auth that meet section 11, including "sign out everywhere".
- **M7. Security and error reporting:**
  - content security policy;
  - CSRF and clickjacking protection;
  - rate limits;
  - whether any error reporting is used, and how it's kept free of user data.
- **M8. Export:** generated on the server or in the browser, size limits, and how long it takes for the 100,000-hand account.
- **M9. Shared definitions:** where the stat definitions and help text live so both apps use one source.
- **M10. Timezone:** whether any server-side aggregation needs the user's time zone to match the browser-side grouping.
