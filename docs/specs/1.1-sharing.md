## 1. Problem

Players want to show a good session, or their progress, to friends and poker groups. Today the only way is a screenshot of the app. Screenshots:
- **Show too much.** Session detail shows the date and start time, buy-in, rebuys, cash-out, $/hr and the hand log. The navigation title is the stakes and game type, and the location appears with the session. A screenshot shares all of it, including how much money the player has.
- **Look bad.** They're cropped to the phone, they include the status bar and tab bar, and the dark UI isn't laid out for a feed or a Story.
- **Carry no branding.** A player bragging about their session doesn't spread the word about PokerStats.

1.1 should make a clean, private-by-default image that the player chooses to send.

---

## 2. Goals and non-goals

### Goals
1. From a completed session, a player can make a good-looking share image in two taps and send it anywhere the iOS share sheet goes.
2. **Private by default.** With no changes, the image shows no dollar amounts, location, date or time, stakes, or other players' names.
3. The player sees exactly what will be shared before sharing, and can turn individual fields on or off.
4. Nothing leaves the device except the image the player sends, through the app they choose.
5. The image is readable and accessible: good contrast, never color alone, and a text description available.
6. The image fits Instagram Stories, iMessage and X without awkward cropping.

### Non-goals for 1.1
- Share links, web pages, hosted images, or any server involvement (owner direction).
- Sharing with other PokerStats users inside the app, following, feeds, comments, or likes.
- Leaderboards (1.3, separate spec).
- Exporting data (CSV/JSON, #9).
- Sharing an **active** session (privacy at the table; see 4.4).
- Sharing an individual hand or the hand log.
- Video, animated, or Live Photo shares.
- Editing the image (stickers, text, filters). The player can do that in the destination app.
- Analytics on shares (unless the owner decides otherwise; open question 7).

---

## 3. User stories

1. As a player who just had a great session, I tap Share on the end-of-session summary, see a clean card with my result in big blinds, and post it to my Instagram Story.
2. As a player in a group chat with friends I play with, I share a session to iMessage and choose to show the dollar result, because they know my game.
3. As a privacy-minded player, I can share a card that shows my play style and VPIP/PFR, with no money and no casino name, without having to turn anything off.
4. As a player who mostly plays at one casino, I'm never at risk of the app revealing where or when I play unless I turn it on.
5. As a player who tracked who sat at my table, I know those names never appear in a share.
6. As a player using larger text or VoiceOver, I can understand the preview and copy a text description to add as alt text where the destination app supports it.
7. As a player who shares often, the app remembers my preferred format and look, but never silently remembers that I once showed my dollar amounts.
8. (Later, not v1) As a player, I share a 30-day summary of my sessions and stats.

## 4. What can be shared

### 4.1 Options considered

| Card | What it shows | Value | Risk and cost |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Session card** | One completed session: result, duration, hands, a few stats, play style | Highest. Matches the moment a player wants to share (just finished) | Lowest. One session's data, already shown on one screen |
| Period summary | Sessions, hours, results and stats over a Trends period (30d, 90d, 6mo, all time) | Good for "my month" posts | Totals across mixed stakes don't convert to big blinds cleanly; bigger privacy surface (volume and frequency of play) |
| Stats snapshot | Lifetime VPIP / PFR / C-bet / WTSD, play style, maybe Leak Finder rating | Bragging about style rather than money | Small. But a stats-only card is more interesting next to leaderboards (1.3) |

### 4.2 Recommendation: minimal v1 = the session card only

- Ship the **session card** in 1.1.
- Design the card builder (preview, toggles, formats, rendering) so a **period summary** card can follow in a 1.1.x update without a new flow. That card is sketched in section 5.3 so the decisions can be made once.
- The stats snapshot waits for 1.3, so it can be designed together with leaderboard profiles.

### 4.3 Where sharing starts (v1)

- **End-of-session summary** (`EndSessionSummaryView`): a "Share" button after a session is ended. This is the main moment.
- **Session detail** (`SessionDetailView`): a share button in the toolbar, next to the existing toolbar items, for any completed session.
- Not in v1: Dashboard, Trends, History rows (long-press), widgets, or the Live Activity.

### 4.4 What can't be shared
- **Active sessions.** No share button while a session is running. Sharing live would broadcast where the player is and how they're doing at that moment.
- Sessions in Recently Deleted (1.1 sync).

## 5. What each card shows

### 5.1 Session card fields and defaults

"Default" is what the card shows when the share flow opens. The player can change anything marked "Toggle" in the preview.

| Field | Source in today's app | Default | Control | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Result in **big blinds** ("+73.5 BB") | `netProfit` divided by the big blind from the stakes ("1/2" gives 2) | **On** (cash games with recognizable stakes) | Toggle | The headline. Shown with a sign and color, never color alone. |
| Result in **dollars** ("+$147") | `netProfit` | **Off** | Toggle | Net result only. Turning it on never adds buy-in, rebuys, add-ons, cash-out or tips. |
| **Stakes** ("1/2 NL") | `stakes`, `gameType` | **Off** | Toggle | Off by default because stakes plus a BB result reveals the dollar amount. When dollars are on, stakes add nothing more and the warning in 5.2 doesn't apply. |
| Game type ("Cash Game") | `gameType.displayName` | On | Toggle | Harmless on its own. |
| **Duration** ("4h 12m") | `duration` | On | Toggle | |
| BB per hour ("+17.5 BB/hr") | `netProfit`, big blind, `durationHours` | On | Toggle | Shown only when the BB result is available and the session is longer than 30 minutes, to avoid silly rates. |
| Hourly rate in dollars ("$34.50/hr") | `hourlyRate` | Off | Follows the dollars toggle | Never shown when dollars are off. |
| Hands logged ("212 hands") | `handCount` | On | Toggle | Labeled "hands logged" in the description, since players don't log every hand. |
| VPIP, PFR | `StatCalculator.computeSessionStats` | On, when at least 20 hands | Toggle (the group) | Percentages, formatted like the app (`ComputedStats.formatPercent`). Hidden below the hand minimum, because small samples look wrong. |
| C-bet, WTSD | Same | Off | Toggle | Kept off to keep the card short. |
| Play style label (TAG, LAG, Nit...) | `PlayStyle.classify(vpip:pfr:)` | On, when at least 20 hands | Toggle | Same label and color as the session detail. |
| **Date** ("Oct 17, 2026") | `startTime` | **Off** | Toggle | Date only. Start and end **times** are never on the card in v1. |
| **Location / casino** | `location` (free text) | **Off** | Toggle | Shown exactly as typed. The preview warns: "Your location will be visible to anyone who sees this image." |
| **Other players' names** (seat names) | `tableConfig` seat `playerName` | **Never** | None | Third-party data. Not available on any card in v1. |
| Notes | `notes` | Never | None | Personal, free text. |
| Mental state (tilt, energy, focus) | `tiltLevel`, `energyLevel`, `focusLevel` | Never in v1 | None | Possible later as a toggle; open question 9. |
| Hand log, positions, Leak Finder insights | Session detail sections | Never in v1 | None | Too detailed for a card. |
| Tips and add-ons (#27), stack checks and P/L chart (#30) | Planned | Never in v1 | None | A small P/L-over-time chart from #30 is a good later addition if it uses BB, not dollars. |
| Branding | n/a | Always | None in v1 | Section 8. |

**When big blinds aren't available**, the BB result, BB/hr and their toggles are hidden, and the card shows no result unless the player turns on dollars. This happens when:
- the stakes are custom text the app can't read (stakes come from the 1/2, 1/3, 2/5, 5/10, 10/20 presets, or free text after choosing "Custom");
- the session is a tournament or sit-and-go (big blinds aren't meaningful there; tournament cards come with #8).

The preview says: "Result in big blinds isn't available for these stakes."

**Sessions with no hands logged:** the stats, play style and hands rows are hidden, and the card shows the result and duration.

**Losing sessions:** shown the same way as winning ones, with the minus sign and loss color. The app doesn't hide or soften losses. A player who doesn't want to share a loss simply doesn't share.

### 5.2 Privacy warnings in the preview

- Turning on **dollars**: none needed. It's the player's choice and they see it.
- Turning on **stakes** while the BB result is shown: inline note "Stakes and big blinds together show your dollar result."
- Turning on **location**: the warning in the table above.
- Turning on **date and location together**: one combined note, "This shows where and when you played."

### 5.3 Period summary card (sketch for the 1.1.x follow-up, not v1)

- Period: the same choices as Trends (30d, 90d, 6mo, all time).
- Default on: sessions played, hours played, percentage of winning sessions, VPIP/PFR and play style (at least 100 hands).
- Default off: total dollars, dollar hourly rate, locations, the period's dates (the card says "Last 30 days" rather than a date range).
- BB total: only if every session in the period has readable stakes, as the sum of each session's BB result, and labeled as such. Otherwise not offered.
- Same toggles, preview, formats and rules as the session card.

## 6. Per-share toggles and remembered preferences

Recommendation: **remember the look, never the exposure.**

| Kind of choice | Examples | Behavior |
|---|---|---|
| Look and layout | Format (Square, Story), theme (dark, light), which stats show (VPIP/PFR, C-bet/WTSD), duration, hands, play style | **Remembered** for the next share |
| Sensitive fields | Dollars, stakes, date, location | **Reset to off every time** the share flow opens. Turning one on applies only to this share. |

- Preferences are stored **on this iPhone only** and don't sync in 1.1 (sharing is an iPhone feature, and a sensitive choice made on one device shouldn't follow the player). Open question 3 asks whether to offer "Always show dollar amounts" in Settings.
- A "Reset to private defaults" button in the preview sets everything back to the table in 5.1.

## 7. Preview before sharing

The share flow is one sheet:

1. **Preview** of the exact image at the top, scaled to fit. What's shown is what's sent, pixel for pixel.
2. **Format** control: Square / Story.
3. **Theme** control: Dark / Light.
4. **Show on card** toggles, grouped:
   - Result: Big blinds, Dollars
   - Session: Game type, Stakes, Duration, Hands logged, Date, Location
   - Stats: VPIP and PFR, C-bet and WTSD, Play style
5. Buttons: **"Share..."** (opens the system share sheet with the image) and **"Copy Description"** (section 9).
6. Cancel in the toolbar.

Rules:
- The preview updates immediately when a toggle changes, with no visible delay on an iPhone 16e [verify].
- Nothing is shared or saved until the player taps "Share..." and picks a destination in the system sheet.
- The share sheet receives **only the image**: no text, no link, no file of session data. Some destinations (Messages, X) would otherwise post the text along with it.
- "Save Image" appears in the system share sheet; there is no separate in-app save button in v1.
- Dismissing the share sheet without sharing returns to the preview with the choices kept.
- The flow works offline and in airplane mode.

## 8. Watermark and branding

- Every card carries a small **"PokerStats" wordmark** in a corner and the line "Tracked with PokerStats" at the bottom, in the secondary text color.
- **No URL, QR code, App Store link or handle** in v1. Owner direction is no links; a printed App Store URL is a link in practice. Open question 5 asks whether to allow plain text such as "PokerStats on the App Store".
- The branding can't be turned off in v1. Open question 6.
- The branding is never larger than the result and never covers data. In the Story format it sits inside the safe area (section 11).
- Visual language matches the app: `PokerTheme` colors (profit green, loss red, accent blue), card corners and spacing, and the same play style colors as `PlayStyleLabelView`.

## 9. Accessibility

### In the app
- The preview sheet, toggles and buttons support Dynamic Type up to the largest accessibility sizes, and VoiceOver.
- VoiceOver reads the preview as one element using the card description below, for example: "Session card preview. Cash Game. Plus 73.5 big blinds. 4 hours 12 minutes. 212 hands logged. VPIP 24 percent, PFR 19 percent. Play style TAG. PokerStats."

### In the image
- **Alt text.** An image passed to the share sheet can't carry alt text into other apps in a reliable way [verify per destination]. So:
  - "Copy Description" copies the same text description to the clipboard, so the player can paste it as alt text (X and Instagram support adding alt text in their composers).
  - The description contains exactly the fields visible on the card, never more.
  - It's never attached to the share automatically (see section 7).
- **Text size.** The card's text scales with the player's Dynamic Type setting, up to a cap that still fits the format [verify the cap on an iPhone 16e]. At large sizes the card drops the lower-priority rows (C-bet/WTSD, then hands, then duration) rather than truncating or shrinking text. The result is never dropped. The preview shows exactly which rows made it.
- **Minimum sizes.** In the exported image, no text is smaller than about 28 px at 1080 px width, so it survives the destination's compression [verify by viewing on X and in Stories].
- **Contrast.** All text meets WCAG AA contrast (4.5:1, or 3:1 for large text) in both themes. The secondary text color in today's dark theme (white at 55 percent opacity) must be checked against the card background.
- **Not color alone.** Results always carry a + or minus sign, and the play style always shows its name, not just its color.
- **Reduce Transparency and Increase Contrast** settings are respected in the rendered card (solid backgrounds, stronger borders).

## 10. Dark and light mode

- The app is dark-only today (the app forces dark appearance at the root).
- Cards come in two themes:
  - **Dark (default):** the app's look, `pokerBackground` and `pokerCard` colors.
  - **Light:** a light background with darker profit and loss colors tuned for contrast; same layout.
- The theme is a choice in the preview, not tied to the system appearance, because the image is viewed on other people's devices. The last choice is remembered (section 6).
- Both themes must pass the contrast checks in section 9.

## 11. Image size for Instagram Stories, iMessage and X

Two formats in v1:

| Format | Pixels | Best for | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Square (default)** | 1080 x 1080 | iMessage, X, Instagram feed, WhatsApp, Discord | X and iMessage show a square image whole without cropping [verify on current X apps]. |
| **Story** | 1080 x 1920 (9:16) | Instagram and Facebook Stories, Snapchat, TikTok photo | Keep all content and branding out of roughly the top 250 px and bottom 300 px, where Stories places its own UI [verify]. |

- Images are rendered on the device at full pixel size regardless of the phone's screen size.
- File format: PNG for crisp text, kept under about 2 MB. If that's too big, a high-quality JPEG is acceptable [verify sizes with the longest card].
- The image contains **no metadata**: no location, no device, no creation date beyond what iOS adds when it's saved. It's drawn by the app, not taken by the camera.
- A 4:5 portrait format (1080 x 1350, the largest X and Instagram feed show uncropped) is a candidate for later; open question 4.

## 12. Privacy

1. **Nothing leaves the device except the image the player sends.** The app makes the image locally and gives it to the system share sheet. The destination app, chosen by the player, is what sends it.
2. **No server, no upload, no link.** Sharing works the same for players who never sign in, and it doesn't touch Supabase or the 1.1 sync data.
3. **No analytics on shares.** The app has no analytics SDK today, and sharing adds none. The app doesn't record whether, when, where or what was shared, including the destination the share sheet reports back. Changing this is open question 7, and would need a privacy policy and App Privacy label update.
4. **Photo library permission only for Save Image.**
   - The app never asks for Photos access when the share flow opens.
   - If the player picks "Save Image" in the share sheet, iOS asks for **add-only** permission the first time. The app needs an add-only Photos usage description for this, or iOS ends the app when Save Image is chosen [verify]. Proposed text: "PokerStats saves share images you create to your photo library."
   - The app never asks to read the photo library.
5. **Third-party names never appear** (seat names, section 5.1).
6. **Private-by-default fields** (dollars, stakes, date, location) reset to off for each share (section 6).
7. **The clipboard** is used only when the player taps "Copy Description".
8. The privacy policy (#54, #72) gets one paragraph: share images are made on the device, contain only what the player chooses, and aren't sent to PokerStats.

## 13. App Review notes

- **Guideline 5.1.1 (data collection):** no new data collection. The Photos permission is add-only, requested only when the player chooses Save Image, with a clear purpose string.
- **App Privacy label:** unchanged if there's no share analytics.
- **Guideline 5.3 (gaming, gambling):** PokerStats tracks the player's own real-world sessions and has no real-money gaming. A card showing results is the player's own record. Keep the card factual, with no encouragement to gamble ("Win big!" and similar copy is out). Check that the current age rating still fits [verify the current rating in App Store Connect].
- **Guideline 1.2 (user-generated content):** doesn't apply. There's no in-app sharing between users, so no moderation or reporting is needed in 1.1. It will apply to 1.3 leaderboards.
- **Guideline 4.0 (design):** the share sheet is the standard system sheet. The share button uses the standard share icon.
- **Review notes text** (for the 1.1 submission): "1.1 adds image sharing of completed sessions through the system share sheet. Images are created on the device. Money, location, date and stakes are hidden by default. No data is sent to our servers."
- **Screenshots:** if a share card appears in App Store screenshots, use seeded demo data, never a real player's data.

## 14. Interaction with 1.3 leaderboards

Leaderboards get their own spec. Sharing in 1.1 must not prejudge it:
- Sharing a card **doesn't** opt anyone into leaderboards, public profiles, or any server feature. Leaderboards will be a separate, explicit opt-in.
- The privacy rules here (big blinds and percentages by default, dollars, location and dates off) are the recommended starting defaults for leaderboard visibility too, so players see one consistent idea of "private by default".
- The card builder and its fields are meant to be reused for a future "share my leaderboard rank" card. That card is out of scope here.
- If 1.3 adds profiles or links, the "no links on cards" rule (section 8) is revisited then, not in 1.1.
- Stats on cards use the same calculations as the app (`StatCalculator`), so a number on a card matches the app and, later, the web and leaderboards (1.1 sync spec, section 12).

## 15. Acceptance criteria (testable)

1. A completed session's End Session summary and Session detail each offer Share. An active session shows no share option anywhere.
2. Opening the share flow with no changes, for a 1/2 cash session with 212 hands, +$147, 4h 12m, at "Bellagio" on Oct 17: the card shows "+73.5 BB", "Cash Game", "4h 12m", "+17.5 BB/hr", "212 hands", VPIP, PFR and the play style. It shows **no** "$", "1/2", "Bellagio", "Oct 17" or any time of day.
3. Turning on Dollars shows "+$147" and "$35.00/hr" in both the preview and the shared image. Buy-in, rebuys and cash-out never appear.
4. Seat names entered in table setup never appear on any card, in any combination of toggles, or in the copied description.
5. With custom stakes the app can't read, or a tournament session, no BB result or BB/hr is shown and the preview explains why. With dollars off, no result is shown.
6. Below 20 hands, stats and play style are hidden. With 0 hands, the hands row is hidden too.
7. Sensitive toggles (dollars, stakes, date, location) are off every time the flow opens, even if they were on last time. Format, theme and stat choices are remembered on this iPhone.
8. The shared image is pixel-identical to the preview (snapshot test). Square is exactly 1080 x 1080 px and Story exactly 1080 x 1920 px. Story content sits inside the safe area.
9. The share sheet receives exactly one item, the image: no text, URL or data file.
10. With airplane mode on, the whole flow works, including Save Image.
11. A network monitor during sharing shows no requests made by PokerStats.
12. The first "Save Image" triggers the add-only Photos prompt. Opening the flow or sharing to Messages triggers no Photos prompt. Declining the prompt doesn't crash the app and doesn't break later shares to other destinations.
13. "Copy Description" copies text that lists exactly the fields visible on the card. VoiceOver reads the same description for the preview.
14. At the largest Dynamic Type setting, the card shows the result without truncation and drops lower-priority rows as specified. The preview sheet controls are all reachable.
15. Text contrast in both themes meets WCAG AA (automated check on the rendered images).
16. The exported image carries no location or other EXIF metadata.
17. Stats on a card equal the same stats on the Session detail screen for the same session.
18. A player who has never signed in can share, with identical behavior.
19. Making the preview after a toggle change takes under 200 ms on an iPhone 16e [verify target].

## 16. Open questions for the owner

1. **Minimal v1:** is the session card alone right for 1.1, with the period summary in a 1.1.x update and the stats snapshot in 1.3?
2. **Defaults:** approve the defaults in 5.1: BB result, game type, duration, BB/hr, hands, VPIP/PFR and play style on; dollars, stakes, date and location off; seat names, notes and mental state never.
3. **Remembered dollars:** should there be a Settings option "Always show dollar amounts on share cards", or should dollars always reset to off as proposed?
4. **Formats:** Square and Story only in v1, or add 4:5 portrait (1080 x 1350)?
5. **Branding text:** should the card say "PokerStats on the App Store" or similar plain text, or stay link-free with just the wordmark?
6. **Removing the watermark:** never, or later as an option (for example a future paid tier)?
7. **Analytics:** confirm no share analytics. If the owner wants a count of shares, it changes the privacy policy and App Privacy label.
8. **Big blinds for custom stakes:** hide the BB result as proposed, or let the player type the big blind in the preview?
9. **Mental state:** should tilt, energy and focus ever be shareable (some players like showing they stayed calm)?
10. **Light theme:** ship it in v1, or dark only to match the app?
11. **Entry points:** End Session summary and Session detail only, or also a long-press in History?
12. **Age rating and gambling:** confirm the current age rating and that showing results on share cards is acceptable for the brand.
